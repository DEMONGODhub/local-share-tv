# Demo video script (target 2:30, hard limit 3:00)

Record the TV (emulator or Fire TV) full screen, and the phone with the Android screen recorder. Cut together, voice-over in English, no music with copyright.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:15 | TV home screen with the QR code | "Your phone has the videos. Your TV has the big screen. Only the TV needs an app: one scan and nothing is downloaded." |
| 0:15-0:40 | Phone: open the normal camera, scan the TV code, tap the link, tap **Choose a whole folder** | "No app on the phone. I scan, open the page and choose a folder." |
| 0:40-1:00 | TV: the shared folder appears | "The TV instantly lists what I shared. Only that, nothing else." |
| 1:00-1:45 | Open a video, play, skip with the remote, show the controls, close and reopen to show resume | "Videos stream straight from the phone. It remembers where I stopped, and plays the folder as a playlist." |
| 1:45-2:05 | Photos full screen, press OK for the slideshow | "Photos work too, with a slideshow." |
| 2:05-2:25 | Phone: tap Stop sharing, TV list clears; mention token-protected QR | "The phone owner stays in control: stop sharing any time." |
| 2:25-2:40 | Tech slide or terminal: Kotlin, Compose, Media3, a pure-Java relay server in the TV app | "Built with Kotlin, Compose, Media3 ExoPlayer, and a dependency-free relay server inside the TV app." |

Checklist: public on YouTube, English, under 3:00, shows the app running on the TV device/emulator.
